The city has become overwhelmed due to the increasing number of traffic incidents and road 
accidents in the area. To better manage this situation, the city council has decided to 
maintain a database containing drivers’ information, including their work are a, most 
frequently visited locations, and whether they have received any driving tickets. If a driver 
has a ticket, the database should store when and where the ticket was issued. 
The council has decided to classify drivers into different categories: 
1. Age-Based Categories 
• Youth: 16–28 years 
• Middle-aged: Above 28 to 50 years 
• Senior/Old age: Above 50 years 
2. Work-Based Categories 
• Student 
• Government employee 
• Self-employed 
• Business owner 
• Private sector employee 
3. Experience-Based Categories 
• New drivers: 0–5 years of experience 
• Moderately experienced drivers: 5–15 years 
• Highly experienced drivers: More than 15 years 
4. Medical Condition Categories 
• Fit 
• Vision impaired 
• Upper extremity impairment 
• Locomotor disability 
 
Database Requirements 
• The system should allow insertion of driver records at the beginning, middle, or end 
based on search criteria (such as county). 
• The city council observed that most accidents involve drivers who recently obtained 
their licenses. Therefore, the system must support retrieving the N most recently 
issued licenses, where N is user-configurable. 
• The council also wants to monitor elderly drivers with possible medical conditions. 
Hence, the system should support retrieving the N oldest issued licenses. 
• When drivers migrate to another city, their records should be removed from the active 
database and moved to a separate inactive drivers database. 
• Because the database can grow very large, the council requires very fast search 
performance, ideally O(1) in most cases. 
 
Class design 
Use composition and inheritance wherever they are appropriate in your design. For instance, 
represent the date of birth using a Date class instead of a string, and represent the address 
using an Address class rather than a string. You should design a base Driver class and derive 
different categories of drivers from it using inheritance. ” 
Design appropriate data members and member functions for a class to support city 
requirements.
